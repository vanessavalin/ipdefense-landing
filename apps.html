The Threats

What's targeting your app's growth?

As apps scale, bad actors follow. These are the four threats app teams face most — and the ones we resolve.

Copycat Apps & Lookalikes

Near-identical names, icons, and screenshots built to intercept your store traffic. Every install they capture is a subscriber taken from you.

Keyword & Search Abuse

Bad actors stuffing and bidding on your branded keywords — taxing your ad spend and hijacking the searches that should convert to your app.

Rogue APK & Mod Sites

Third-party sites distributing modified or repackaged versions of your app — bypassing your paywall and exposing users to malware under your name.

Social Impersonation

Fake "official" accounts scamming your community on Instagram, TikTok, and X. You lose the revenue today — and the user's trust forever.

Common Questions

From app teams like yours.

Can you remove copycat apps from the App Store and Google Play?

Yes — copycat and impersonator app removal is IP Defense's core specialty. We identify infringing apps, verify the violation, prepare the evidence each store requires, and file through the correct channel. Apple and Google make the final call; we manage the case and escalation throughout.

How fast are takedowns?

Clear App Store cases typically resolve in 2–4 weeks; Google Play in 3–5. We manage every escalation until close.

What happens to the infringing developer?

Outcomes vary by case and platform: listings removed, apps forced to rebrand, branded-keyword abuse stopped, or in some cases the developer account terminated at Apple's or Google's discretion. Where removal isn't the right remedy, a licensing settlement is sometimes the better outcome.

How is this different from a takedown tool?

Automated tools blast templated complaints and hope they stick. IP Defense verifies each app, tailors the complaint to the store's specific requirements, and follows up through escalation — which is what turns a filing into an actual removal instead of a rejection.
