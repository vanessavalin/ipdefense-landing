Privacy Policy

How IPDefense collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: July 8, 2026

IPDefense ("we," "us," or "our") provides managed brand protection services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website at ipdefense.ai, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We have written it to describe what our site actually does, in plain language.

Information You Give Us

The only information we collect directly from you is what you voluntarily submit through our risk audit request form. That includes:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your company name
  • Your work email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • App Store, Google Play, or website URLs associated with your brand
  • Any details you choose to share about your brand protection situation

We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry, prepare your risk audit, and communicate with you about our services. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website does not use cookies, advertising pixels, analytics scripts, or third-party tracking technologies. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not follow you across other websites.

Third-Party Services

A small number of third parties are involved in delivering our website. Each has its own privacy practices, which we encourage you to review:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may log standard technical information such as IP addresses as part of serving requests.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on this site. Loading these fonts involves a request to Google's servers.
  • Google Workspace receives and stores the contents of submitted forms so our team can respond to you.
  • Apple's public iTunes lookup service is queried when you enter an App Store URL in our form, so we can confirm the listing you are referring to. Only the app identifier from the URL you typed is sent.

How We Store and Protect Information

Form submissions are stored in access-controlled Google Workspace accounts available only to team members who need them to respond to your inquiry. We apply least-access principles: people see only what their role requires. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How Long We Keep It

We retain inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and, where a business relationship follows, for as long as necessary to provide our services and meet legal or record-keeping obligations. You may ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to access it, correct it, request its deletion, object to or restrict how we use it, request a portable copy, and withdraw consent you previously gave.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, these rights arise under the General Data Protection Regulation. If you are in Canada, they arise under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. Because we do not sell personal information, there is nothing to opt out of in that respect.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

International Transfers

We operate in North America, and the service providers described above may process information in the United States and other countries. Where information is transferred across borders, we rely on the safeguards offered by those providers and applicable legal transfer mechanisms.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated through this page.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or want to exercise a privacy right? Reach us through the contact form on our homepage, and we will get back to you.
